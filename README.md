# Pak Kopi ☕

**Your next kopi, just a few taps away.**

Explore the menu, customise your drink, and order ahead for pickup. Behind the counter, staff can manage preparation and collection, while an admin dashboard brings products, availability, and orders together.

## Take a look

🌿 **[Explore the brand website](https://pakkopi.vercel.app)**  
🥤 **[Try the ordering app](https://pak-kopi-order.vercel.app)**

## From first sip to final pickup

- Choose a branch and find your favourite drink.
- Make it yours with drink options.
- Place an order and follow its progress.
- Keep the café moving with staff POS and collection boards.

Built independently with help from AI coding tools, from the customer interfaces to the shared backend and ordering workflows.

*A personal demo pitched to Pak Kopi, not a live business system. Payments are simulated.*
